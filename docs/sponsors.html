FAQ

How do I sponsor OpenSyntax?

Use the sponsor link above or contact It Is Unique Official to discuss sponsorship options.

How will my name appear?

Sponsors are listed by display name, tier, website or social link, and an optional short note.

Can companies sponsor?

Yes. Companies can sponsor as Builder, Partner, or Enterprise Sponsor depending on support level and recognition needs.

How do I update sponsor details?

Contact It Is Unique Official with the requested name, link, logo, tier, or removal update.
